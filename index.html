More Views, Different Answer: Monotone Consistency in Vision-Language Models

F. Al-Amin Dipto*, A. Das, O. Hoque

Under review, VLM4RWD Workshop, NeurIPS 2026

Defines monotone consistency for visual preprocessing: if a model is confident given a set of views, its answer should not change when given a larger set that byte-exactly contains every view it already had. Across 4,588 unselected multiple-choice items and five checkpoints, four models break the property strictly. Introduces V@q, a cross-model correction that reports violations on the top q of each model's own margin distribution; the correction reverses one cross-model conclusion.

Answer When Supported, Abstain When Not: Pass-Aware Calibration for Verifier-Gated RAG

F. Al-Amin Dipto*, A. B. Shuaib, R. Shahriyar

In submission, ACL Rolling Review, October 2026 cycle

A separately trained 7B QLoRA verifier scores a candidate answer against retrieved context and routes on a calibrated first-token-logit threshold. Pass-aware threshold selection with a stratified balanced hard-negative scheme improves balanced score by +0.17 (p < 10-4, paired bootstrap) over a zero-shot gate, matches Gemini-2.5 Flash/Pro, transfers without retuning to TriviaQA and Natural Questions, and raises negative abstention from 0.03 to 0.98 under a wrong-span stress test.

Output-Style, Not Capability: Why Strict-EM Misranks Cross-Lingual VLMs

F. Al-Amin Dipto*, O. Hoque, R. Shahriyar

In submission, ACL Rolling Review, October 2026 cycle

Strict exact-match scoring misranks cross-lingual document-VQA models by rewarding output-style priors over capability: ranking reversals occur in 99.8% of paired-bootstrap resamples across Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Llama-3.2-Vision-11B, InternVL and Phi. Proposes a vision-grounded semantic-EM judge, cross-checked against a second judge and 50 blind human annotations, and releases CLP, a nine-item reporting protocol for cross-lingual VLM evaluation.

CoMedBench: A Multi-Source Benchmark of Synthetic Medical Data Fidelity and Downstream Utility

A. Das, F. Al-Amin Dipto, M. S. Anto, D. Rehkopf, A. Vala, T. S. Pias

Preprint, under review at AAAI · arXiv:2608.12805

A reproducible benchmark evaluating synthetic-data generators under one clinical-validity framework and a shared training and evaluation engine: 37 dataset-task pairs (20 static tabular, 17 temporal ICU time-series) across seven public sources including MIMIC-III, MIMIC-IV, eICU, NHANES and CDC BRFSS. CoMed-TVAE retains 97.3% mean AUROC synthetic-to-real utility on tabular tasks; temporal ICU tasks are harder and more generator-sensitive.

Ongoing Work

StochGuard: verifying LLM-generated stochastic GPU kernels

An open-source verifier that checks a declared coupling contract rather than a reference output, for a setting where exact-match evaluation rejects correct kernels and mean-statistic checks accept wrong ones. Calibrated over 96,000 GH200 runs with zero false rejections and all 11 injected fault classes detected; 0 of 41 generated kernels drawing on PyTorch's own generator passed exact-output verification. Pre-registered gates and adversarial controls surfaced four defects in my own instruments, including a 70% false-negative rate since repaired.
